# The self-tests (tests/)

What each self-test file covers. How to run them, the tiers and the rules for new tests are in docs/testing.md.


- `AudioSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the offline audio checks behind `--audio-selftest`.
- `ProjectSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the TOML, project-file, theme, history, export-name and autosave checks behind `--project-selftest`, and the automation lanes' and automation recording's (`AutomationWrite`, and `AutomationWriter` with a fake playhead: armed, playing, one undo step; for the master: `lanesAt`/`editLanesAt` and `masterAutomationRows` on master places, a pass writing the volume and a master effect's knob as one step, disarming, and the strip's value following the playhead).
- `BridgeSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the audio bridge checks (`runBridgeChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`) with a deviceless engine: a track's processor survives a delete, undo, reorder, a kind change and edits, and only the track that changed is made or removed.
- `MarkersSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the markers checks (`runMarkersChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): sorting and finding them around a beat, ids, the file round trip, the undo labels and an undo through the store. The render suite checks the markers lane by input (`markerEditChecks`: adding, locating, moving, the menus, the ruler's flags) and draws them in the `tempo` and `tempo-hidden` scenes.
- `MidiImportSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the MIDI import checks (`runMidiImportChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): a file `writeMidiFile` wrote read back (notes, controllers, tempo and signatures), hand-made bytes (running status, velocity-0 offs, overlapping notes on a key, a type 0 file split by channel), refusals (not MIDI, SMPTE, type 2, a track cut short, a length that lies), parts onto tracks, and `fileTiming`.
- `TakesSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the takes and comping checks (`runTakesChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): slicing, comping audio and MIDI ranges, click ranges, boundaries, stepping, folding a recording into takes, removing and flattening, the file round trip and ids, the undo labels; loop recording three laps of audio and MIDI through `RecordingSession` on a deviceless engine (`AudioSystem::setOfflineInputs`), each lap a take at its sample; and the comp played through `AudioBridge`, crossfading where sections meet, and an auditioned lane heard instead. The render suite checks comping by input (`takeEditChecks`) and draws the `takes` scene.
- `TempoSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the tempo map checks (`runTempoChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): `audio::TempoMap`'s conversions, bars and tidying, and the model around it (the file keys, the undo labels, clips, export ranges and the MIDI conductor). The audio suite checks the engine's side (the playhead keeping its beat, a note after a tempo change, the metronome in 6/8) and the render suite the lanes (`tempoEditChecks`, the `tempo` scene).
- `RoutingSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the routing checks (`runRoutingChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`; see the project self-test above; its `sidechain*Checks` cover the keys).
- `InputSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the signal, event bus, keymap and text-field checks behind `--input-selftest`. With `VYBN_TEST_BENCH` it also times dispatch (`benchDispatch`: an emit through 32 hooks, a publish to 8 subscribers, a post and its dispatch, an environment loop iteration with 16 posted actions).
- `NoteSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the note tool checks behind `--notes-selftest`.
- `StepSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the step sequencer's checks (`StepSequence`: the grid, steps, rows, swing, patterns, the GM kit and drum names, the rows shown), run from `--notes-selftest` through `runNoteSelfTest` (`runStepChecks()`).
- `EngineSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the scheduler, real-time pool, parallel mixing and streaming checks behind `--engine-selftest` (it writes its sample and cache in a temporary folder it removes, so two engine suites can't run at once). With `VYBN_TEST_BENCH` it also times the pool and the scheduler (`benchPoolAndScheduler`: 24-job batches back to back and paced at 5.3 ms, with the system timer at 1 ms so the pace holds, and the workers' CPU cycles; 4 s of 24 tracks mixed on one thread and on four workers; the cost of `wake` and its latency).
- `SynthSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the synthesizer checks behind `--synth-selftest` (see Commands): the wave bank, pitch, envelopes, filters, unison, voices, LFOs, the mod wheel, output limits, the timed render, and a note through a track's chain (`makeInstrumentDevice`, `ChainInstrument`, `NotePlayer`), and the 24 factory presets (unique names, values known and in range, each playing a C major chord to a finite peak under 2, a preset's setting named after it).
- `ExportSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the mastering and encoder checks behind `--export-selftest` (see Commands): the K-weighting coefficients, loudness and gating, true peak, the limiter, dither, fades, MD5, the WAV, AIFF and FLAC round trips, MP3 (quality per channel at the decoder's delay, the ID3 tag and its gapless frame, bitrate, mono, UTF-16 titles, refusals, a dropped writer, and an MP3 target rendered against its WAV), FLAC's signature, metadata, refusals and failure paths, and the float temp files, and the MIDI writer (checked by a small SMF reader in the test: tempo, time signature, notes and their cuts, controllers, pitch bend, ranges, channels), and the offline engine and render queue (see `RenderJob`).
- `PluginSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the plugin checks behind `--plugin-selftest`, with `tests/plugins/TestPlugins.cpp` (plain CLAP, no helpers): `vybn.test.gain` (Gain in dB, Mute, a read-only Peak it reports each block, Latency 0-4096 frames that really delays its sound and is reported through the latency extension after a restart, state as text, and an interface, floating or embedded, whose left half takes 1 dB off and right half adds 1 dB as a gesture), `vybn.test.sine` (sine voices for CLAP or MIDI notes, Level; it ignores a wildcard note off, as Surge XT does) `vybn.test.faulty` (Fault: crash, hang or 60 ms a block; at creation it tries to write a file in the user's profile and beside its own file, and logs whether it could), `vybn.test.duck` (a stereo effect with a stereo sidechain input port that isn't the main one: its output is its input times 1 minus the key's peak in the block, so a key at full scale silences it) and `vybn.test.crashinit` (crashes when it is initialised: the scan lists it and only its sidechain probe dies).
- `Vst3SelfTest.h/.cpp`: the plugin suite's VST3 checks, with `tests/plugins/TestVst3.cpp` (written against the VST3 interface headers alone, built as the bundle `output/plugins/vybn-test-plugins.vst3`): VST Gain (Gain 0-1 shown as -60 to +12 dB, Mute one step, a read-only Peak it reports as an output change, Latency eight steps of 512 frames that delays its sound and asks for a restart, state, and an editor whose left half steps Gain down and right half up through the host), VST Sine (an event input; Level, and Bend and Mod driven by the bend and controller 1 through its MIDI mapping), VST Duck (an aux input as its sidechain; its edit controller a class of its own) and Test Gain, a twin of the CLAP gain's name and vendor for the catalogue's choice between formats.
- `BrowserSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the JSON, listing, zip, SFZ and library checks behind `--browser-selftest`. It builds zip files by hand (deflating with `stb_image_write.h`'s compressor, compiled into it alone) and writes everything in a temporary folder it removes.
- `RenderSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the golden-image checks behind `--render-selftest` (see Commands): the scenes rendered off screen, each compared with its picture in `tests/render/` (`<scene>.png`, opaque RGB, written by `--update-goldens`), and the synth and waveforms scenes' 50-frame timings. Among the scenes, `automation` is the arrangement with a track's lanes shown, and `automationEditChecks` edits a lane by input handed to a laid-out ArrangementView (add, drag, neighbours, double-click and right-click removal, the add strip, the lane's x); `master` is the arrangement with the master's lanes shown (a deviceParams hook names the effect's lane), and `masterEditChecks` does the same on the master's row (a click adds a point to the project's master lane, drag, removal, the add strip opening the master's menu, the lane's x, and the row never being a track: `trackAt` reports it as the space below, and pressing or dragging it selects, moves and adds nothing); `radial` is a track header's toggles opened as a radial menu (under a `StillContainer`, not an overlay, so the real mouse can't hover it), and `radialToggleChecks` drives an open `RadialMenu` (drag onto a button and let go, let go on the middle then click, the middle closing it); `folding` is the nested-groups arrangement (a golden and a cache scene), `foldChecks` and `foldUiChecks` fold on a laid-out `ArrangementView` (rows below moving up by the hidden rows' heights, `trackAt` never finding a hidden track, the chevron's click, indentation by depth, the folded counts, the selection unfolding once, Fold all and Unfold all, `FoldState`'s version) and `foldMixerChecks` counts a `MixerView`'s strips as groups fold, and `foldDragChecks` opens a folded project (its tracks hidden) and drags headers by input (a folded group moving with its tracks, a track dropped among hidden ones landing before the group); `sidechain` is the Devices panel on a pad with a keyed Compressor (a golden and a cache scene), `sidechainUiChecks` and `sidechainDeviceViewChecks` drive a real `DevicesView` and `DeviceView` (the choices, a choice as one undo step, the caption, renaming, adding and deleting tracks, undo, the master's, the limit's note), and `VYBN_TEST_BENCH` times the offers on a 64-track project (`INFO sidechain bench:`).
