# The self-tests (tests/)

What each self-test file covers. How to run them, the tiers and the rules for new tests are in docs/testing.md.


- `AudioSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the offline audio checks behind `--audio-selftest`.
- `ProjectSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the TOML, project-file, theme, history, export-name and autosave checks behind `--project-selftest`, and the automation lanes' and automation recording's (`AutomationWrite`, and `AutomationWriter` with a fake playhead: armed, playing, one undo step; for the master: `lanesAt`/`editLanesAt` and `masterAutomationRows` on master places, a pass writing the volume and a master effect's knob as one step, disarming, and the strip's value following the playhead).
- `BridgeSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the audio bridge checks (`runBridgeChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`) with a deviceless engine: a track's processor survives a delete, undo, reorder, a kind change and edits, and only the track that changed is made or removed.
- `MarkersSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the markers checks (`runMarkersChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): sorting and finding them around a beat, ids, the file round trip, the undo labels and an undo through the store. The render suite checks the markers lane by input (`markerEditChecks`: adding, locating, moving, the menus, the ruler's flags) and draws them in the `tempo` and `tempo-hidden` scenes.
- `MidiImportSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the MIDI import checks (`runMidiImportChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): a file `writeMidiFile` wrote read back (notes, controllers, tempo and signatures), hand-made bytes (running status, velocity-0 offs, overlapping notes on a key, a type 0 file split by channel), refusals (not MIDI, SMPTE, type 2, a track cut short, a length that lies), parts onto tracks, and `fileTiming`.
- `TakesSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the takes and comping checks (`runTakesChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): slicing, comping audio and MIDI ranges, click ranges, boundaries, stepping, folding a recording into takes, removing and flattening, the file round trip and ids, the undo labels; loop recording three laps of audio and MIDI through `RecordingSession` on a deviceless engine (`AudioSystem::setOfflineInputs`), each lap a take at its sample; and the comp played through `AudioBridge`, crossfading where sections meet, and an auditioned lane heard instead. The render suite checks comping by input (`takeEditChecks`) and draws the `takes` scene.
- `WarpSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): warping's checks (docs/design/warp-plan.md). `runWarpAudioChecks()`, run from the audio suite: the FFT's round trip, the warp map (no markers, one, many, both ways, tidying), the analysis (a click train's sixteen clicks within 2 ms, a 4-bar loop at 120 BPM, a tempo of 97 BPM, a held chord neither percussive nor full of transients), the clip player's warped regions (every mode sample for sample with an unwarped clip at the file's own speed; Repitch at twice the speed; Tones and Complex keeping a 440 Hz sine at 440 Hz stretched by 1.5 and lasting 6 s, and transposed an octave to 880 Hz at its own length; Beats putting each click on its beat at 90 and at 160 BPM; a voice kept across a new sequence with the output unchanged, a voice each for two clips, a jump filled at once; blocks of 4096 the same as of 256; a tempo change followed). `runWarpModelChecks()`, run from the project suite: `setWarp` keeping the sound in place through a tempo change and a ramp, at one tempo with one marker, and off again; the clip's tempo and `fitToLength`; markers added, moved, slipped, removed and cleared; `quantiseAudio` at full and half strength; `applyGuess` with a pickup; split, trim and slice of a warped clip keeping its sound; the file round trip and its refusals, clamping and older files; the saved-state check and the undo labels; and the audio editor's marker strip driven by input (a press making a marker and dragging it, a double-click removing it). The render suite draws it in the `warp` scene.
- `TempoSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the tempo map checks (`runTempoChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`): `audio::TempoMap`'s conversions, bars and tidying, and the model around it (the file keys, the undo labels, clips, export ranges and the MIDI conductor). The audio suite checks the engine's side (the playhead keeping its beat, a note after a tempo change, the metronome in 6/8) and the render suite the lanes (`tempoEditChecks`, the `tempo` scene).
- `RoutingSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the routing checks (`runRoutingChecks()`, run from `ProjectSelfTest`; see the project self-test above; its `sidechain*Checks` cover the keys).
- `InputSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the signal, event bus, keymap and text-field checks behind `--input-selftest`. With `VYBN_TEST_BENCH` it also times dispatch (`benchDispatch`: an emit through 32 hooks, a publish to 8 subscribers, a post and its dispatch, an environment loop iteration with 16 posted actions).
- `NoteSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the note tool checks behind `--notes-selftest`.
- `StepSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the step sequencer's checks (`StepSequence`: the grid, steps, rows, swing, patterns, the GM kit and drum names, the rows shown), run from `--notes-selftest` through `runNoteSelfTest` (`runStepChecks()`).
- `ExpressionSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): notes' expression curves (`runExpressionChecks()`, run from `--notes-selftest` after the step checks): a curve's values, the note tools carrying them (slice, join, chop, stretch, reverse, move, transpose), the tuning lane's edits (`setCurvePoint`, `curveLine`, `shiftCurve`, `tidyCurve`), the history naming an edit, and the file (no key without curves, a round trip, unknown types dropped, values clamped, points sorted).
- `EngineSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the scheduler, real-time pool, parallel mixing and streaming checks behind `--engine-selftest` (it writes its sample and cache in a temporary folder it removes, so two engine suites can't run at once). With `VYBN_TEST_BENCH` it also times the pool and the scheduler (`benchPoolAndScheduler`: 24-job batches back to back and paced at 5.3 ms, with the system timer at 1 ms so the pace holds, and the workers' CPU cycles; 4 s of 24 tracks mixed on one thread and on four workers; the cost of `wake` and its latency).
- `SynthSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the synthesizer checks behind `--synth-selftest` (see Commands): the wave bank, pitch, envelopes, filters, unison, voices, LFOs, the mod wheel, output limits, the timed render, and a note through a track's chain (`makeInstrumentDevice`, `ChainInstrument`, `NotePlayer`), and the 24 factory presets (unique names, values known and in range, each playing a C major chord to a finite peak under 2, a preset's setting named after it).
- `ExportSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the mastering and encoder checks behind `--export-selftest` (see Commands): the K-weighting coefficients, loudness and gating, true peak, the limiter, dither, fades, MD5, the WAV, AIFF and FLAC round trips, MP3 (quality per channel at the decoder's delay, the ID3 tag and its gapless frame, bitrate, mono, UTF-16 titles, refusals, a dropped writer, and an MP3 target rendered against its WAV), FLAC's signature, metadata, refusals and failure paths, and the float temp files, and the MIDI writer (checked by a small SMF reader in the test: tempo, time signature, notes and their cuts, controllers, pitch bend, ranges, channels), and the offline engine and render queue (see `RenderJob`).
- `PluginSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the plugin checks behind `--plugin-selftest`, with `tests/plugins/TestPlugins.cpp` (plain CLAP, no helpers): `vybn.test.gain` (Gain in dB, Mute, a read-only Peak it reports each block, Latency 0-4096 frames that really delays its sound and is reported through the latency extension after a restart, state as text, and an interface, floating or embedded, whose left half takes 1 dB off and right half adds 1 dB as a gesture), `vybn.test.sine` (sine voices for CLAP or MIDI notes, Level; it ignores a wildcard note off, as Surge XT does) `vybn.test.faulty` (Fault: crash, hang or 60 ms a block; at creation it tries to write a file in the user's profile and beside its own file, and logs whether it could), `vybn.test.duck` (a stereo effect with a stereo sidechain input port that isn't the main one: its output is its input times 1 minus the key's peak in the block, so a key at full scale silences it) and `vybn.test.crashinit` (crashes when it is initialised: the scan lists it and only its sidechain probe dies).
- `Vst3SelfTest.h/.cpp`: the plugin suite's VST3 checks, with `tests/plugins/TestVst3.cpp` (written against the VST3 interface headers alone, built as the bundle `output/plugins/vybn-test-plugins.vst3`): VST Gain (Gain 0-1 shown as -60 to +12 dB, Mute one step, a read-only Peak it reports as an output change, Latency eight steps of 512 frames that delays its sound and asks for a restart, state, and an editor whose left half steps Gain down and right half up through the host, saying its state is dirty through `IComponentHandler2`), VST Sine (an event input; Level, and Bend and Mod driven by the bend and controller 1 through its MIDI mapping; its parameters in units, "Voice" and "Pitch" under it; Program, a program change choosing from the root unit's three programs; a note-off ending its note by the note's id), VST Duck (an aux input as its sidechain; its edit controller a class of its own; 64-bit processing only) and Test Gain, a twin of the CLAP gain's name and vendor for the catalogue's choice between formats.
- `BrowserSelfTest.h/.cpp`: the JSON, listing, zip, SFZ and library checks behind `--browser-selftest`. It builds zip files by hand (deflating with `stb_image_write.h`'s compressor, compiled into it alone) and writes everything in a temporary folder it removes.
- `RenderSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the golden-image checks behind `--render-selftest` (see Commands): the scenes rendered off screen, each compared with its picture in `tests/render/` (`<scene>.png`, opaque RGB, written by `--update-goldens`), and the synth and waveforms scenes' 50-frame timings. Among the scenes, `automation` is the arrangement with a track's lanes shown, and `automationEditChecks` edits a lane by input handed to a laid-out ArrangementView (add, drag, neighbours, double-click and right-click removal, the add strip, the lane's x); `master` is the arrangement with the master's lanes shown (a deviceParams hook names the effect's lane), and `masterEditChecks` does the same on the master's row (a click adds a point to the project's master lane, drag, removal, the add strip opening the master's menu, the lane's x, and the row never being a track: `trackAt` reports it as the space below, and pressing or dragging it selects, moves and adds nothing); `radial` is a track header's toggles opened as a radial menu (under a `StillContainer`, not an overlay, so the real mouse can't hover it), and `radialToggleChecks` drives an open `RadialMenu` (drag onto a button and let go, let go on the middle then click, the middle closing it); `folding` is the nested-groups arrangement (a golden and a cache scene), `foldChecks` and `foldUiChecks` fold on a laid-out `ArrangementView` (rows below moving up by the hidden rows' heights, `trackAt` never finding a hidden track, the chevron's click, indentation by depth, the folded counts, the selection unfolding once, Fold all and Unfold all, `FoldState`'s version) and `foldMixerChecks` counts a `MixerView`'s strips as groups fold, and `foldDragChecks` opens a folded project (its tracks hidden) and drags headers by input (a folded group moving with its tracks, a track dropped among hidden ones landing before the group); `browserDragChecks` opens the `AssetBrowser` over an arrangement and drags a built-in instrument's row by input (found with `rowRect`: a move past a few pixels starts one drag with the row's key, a smaller one or a click none) and asks the arrangement for the drop rows (a track, the space under the tracks); `sidechain` is the Devices panel on a pad with a keyed Compressor (a golden and a cache scene), `sidechainUiChecks` and `sidechainDeviceViewChecks` drive a real `DevicesView` and `DeviceView` (the choices, a choice as one undo step, the caption, renaming, adding and deleting tracks, undo, the master's, the limit's note), and `VYBN_TEST_BENCH` times the offers on a 64-track project (`INFO sidechain bench:`).
- `FreezeSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): freezing's model (`runFreezeProjectChecks()`, from the project suite): what the hash follows (the sound and its key's; not the fader, name, mute or selection), the file's round trip and a bad hash refused, the history's labels and an undo through the store. The export suite freezes a track of noise through a saturation (the file as long as it rings, one render per track at a time), exports the live and the frozen songs (the same within -150 dB), bounces it (a new audio track, the source muted), flattens it, and checks an effect's change puts the freeze out of date while the fader doesn't.
- `AnalysisSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): the Analyzer's measurements (`runAnalysisChecks()`, from the audio suite): a tap in order, off, full and with two writers at once (one skips, nothing tears); a 1 kHz sine's band and level, the tilt, the fall and the hold; correlation, width and balance. The render suite's `analyzer` scene draws the panel after four seconds of a fixed chord.
- `MidiEffectsSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): MIDI effects. `runMidiEffectAudioChecks()` (from the audio suite): the arpeggiator's steps on the grid, its gate, down and up-down over octaves, and Hold; a chord's notes and overlapping chords' clean ends; the scale's nearest and down, and A minor; echoes' times, levels, pitches and note-offs; velocity's range and drive; bypass; an arpeggiator before the synth through a `ChainInstrument`, eight notes heard on the grid. `runMidiEffectProjectChecks()` (from the project suite): the file round trip, refusals, the history's labels.
- `ModulationSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): modulators. `runModulationAudioChecks()`: the LFO's shapes, polarity, phase, sync, free rate and its sample and hold; Steps and glide; the follower's times; a routed LFO moving a gain effect's level each block and clamped, and under automation; a macro moving the track's volume. `runModulationProjectChecks()`: the file round trip, an orphan route dropped, a repeated number refused, the history's labels, the freeze hash.
- `TranscribeSelfTest.h/.cpp` (namespace `tests`): audio to MIDI. `runTranscribeChecks()` (audio suite): generated drums named and placed; a sine melody's keys, starts and lengths and a glide kept as tuning; ringing plucks one note each; C, Am and G7 over their beats. `runTranscriptionProjectChecks()` (project suite): converted notes and chords on the clip's beats, warped or not.
